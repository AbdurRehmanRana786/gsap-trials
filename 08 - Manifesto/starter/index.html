Manifesto

§ 1 of 1 · Read aloud, slowly

Type is not decoration. It is the voice a brand speaks in before anyone has read a single word. We draw letters that keep their nerve at six pixels and at sixty metres, that move without wobbling, and that still mean something when they stand perfectly still. A good typeface earns its keep in the paragraph, not just on the poster. The best motion is the kind you feel before you notice it — and the best letters are the ones nobody ever has to think about twice.

Three rules we don’t break

Pinned above every desk in the studio, slightly crooked.

  1. 01

    Legibility is a feeling.

    If a reader has to squint, we have failed — however good it looks on the specimen sheet. Every weight is proofed at nine points, on a cheap phone, on a bright train, before it leaves the studio.

  2. 02

    Motion has manners.

    Letters may dance, but they don’t cut in. Everything we animate gives the eye somewhere to land within six hundred milliseconds, and never moves the word you were halfway through reading.

  3. 03

    Every glyph pays rent.

    If a character can’t justify its place in the family, it goes. Two hundred and twelve styles, and not one ornamental swash that hasn’t earned its keep.
