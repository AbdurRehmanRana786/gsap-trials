01 The Rings

282,000 km wide. Ten metres thick.

Seen edge-on from Earth, they all but vanish. Seen from here, tipped 27° towards the Sun, they are the most expensive vinyl in the solar system — seven grooves of ice, sorted by size, turning at 17 km a second.

02 The Moons

Moons by the hundred. Five worth the detour.

  1. Titan

    Thicker air than Earth, methane rain and lakes you could sail.

  2. Enceladus

    Geysers of salt water, snow brighter than fresh paper.

  3. Mimas

    One crater a third of its own width. It is fine. It is fine.

  4. Iapetus

    One side coal, one side snow. Two-tone since forever.

  5. Hyperion

    A tumbling sponge. Nobody can agree which way is up.

03 Visit

Twelve seats. One very long night.

A night on Saturnine lasts nine Earth days. Bring a jumper — the dome holds at 19 °C, the view does not.
